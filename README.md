
# RAW/HARD/SPECULATIVE

**Seeds. Not novels. Not drafts. Just the spark.**

This is the canonical seed repository for *Raw/Hard/Speculative* — a place for consequential speculative fiction born from current events, tech shifts, and power realignments.

## What you'll find here

- **`/seeds/`** – Outlines, story briefs, world-building fragments. 500 words or less. Unpolished by design.
- No full manuscripts. No requests for line edits. No pull requests.

## How this works

**I write seeds. You read them. That's it.**

If a seed sparks something in you:
1. **Fork this repository** to your own GitHub account.
2. **Write your own version** — expand, remix, burn it down.
3. **Publish it anywhere** — your own GitHub Pages, a blog, Substack, a PDF.
4. **Email me a link** (or open a Discussion) and I may add your fork to the **Hall of Forks** below.

I will never merge your fork back here. Your feifdom is yours.

## Future possibilities (not promises)

- **Private collaboration forks** – Hand-selected maintainers only. If you demonstrate sustained, thoughtful work on your public fork, you may be invited to a shared private repository with 2–4 others. Invitation only. No applications.
- **Maintainer control** – Each invited maintainer owns their branch/namespace. I maintain the root.

But today? Today is just seeds and forks.

## Why this exists

I start my days with coffee and news. When something consequential happens — a rocket explodes, a treaty breaks, a model collapses — I go to AI and speculate. Consequences. Power blocs. Second-order effects.

Sometimes a seed of a story emerges. This is where those seeds land.

Read. Fork. Burn.
