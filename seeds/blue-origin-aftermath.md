# Seed: Blue Origin Aftermath
**Trigger:** New Glenn booster anomaly, explosion on ignition taking out pad infrastruture 
**Tags:** #launch #competition #geopolitics  
Blue Origin loses a booster. No crew, but the payload was a prototype lunar lander for a NASA CLPS mission. 


**Immediate consequences:**  
- ULA and SpaceX scramble to reallocate launch windows for NASA's Artemis timeline.  
- Insurance markets adjust premiums upward for "second-tier" providers.  
- Owner personally calls the NASA Administrator — a scene worth writing.

**Second-order:**  
- European launch providers (Arianespace) see a temporary pricing advantage.  
- Chinese state media runs a quiet "capitalism fails in space" narrative.  
- A Reddit thread correctly predicts the failure mode 48 hours before. The OP is never found.

**Character fragment:**  
> "The feed went to potato-quality right before the RUD," says Tanya, who runs mission analytics for a hedge fund that shorts rocket-adjacent equities. "I made my bonus in forty seconds. Felt dirty. Did it again next week."

## Possible arcs

1. **The investigator** – An FAA analyst discovers the anomaly was sabotage. But who? And why did the telemetry scrub happen *before* the explosion?
2. **The insurance thriller** – A Lloyd's underwriter races to deny a massive claim, only to realize the policy had a "first-stage RUD" exclusion written in invisible ink.
3. **The aftermath comedy** – A low-level Blue Origin comms person has to write the press release while their ex, who now works at SpaceX, keeps sending "u ok?" texts.
4. **The #1 lift provider** ramps up prices as no other medium term challengers
5. **The Chinese program escallates** going direct to market for communication, servers, stations, as they see none else can get to #2 before them.
